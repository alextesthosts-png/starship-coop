Media library

Images and 3D models stored in your Railway bucket. Use them for the announcement banner, the menu background, section text (![caption](link)) and ship models.

Ships & models

Swap the 3D model of any ship or structure, player and pirate alike, for one from the media library. Uploaded models are scaled and centred to the built-in model's size automatically, so hitboxes and gun positions still fit. Players load them the next time they open the game. The inspector shows what the game needs: engine markers (engine_*), gun markers (gun_*), size and materials.

How to prepare a model in Blender
  1. Model the ship with its nose pointing along Blender's +Y axis, about 12–15 m long, origin in the middle.
  2. Add empties named engine_L, engine_R… at each exhaust (custom property plumeRadius sets the plume size) and gun_0, gun_1… at the cannon muzzles.
  3. Bake your materials into one material whose name ends in Baked to keep its look; otherwise name materials …Hull, …Accent (player colour), …Dark, …Canopy, …Glow.
  4. Export glTF Binary (.glb) with +Y up, “Custom Properties” on, and no Draco compression. Upload it to the media library.

Custom ships

Add new playable ships next to the Lancer, Warden and Hammer. A new ship flies and fires like the built-in ship it's based on, with your own name, model, numbers and secondary weapon. Switch it on and it shows up in ship select and the lobby for everyone. Limit it to certain missions if you like.

Liveries & emblems

Paint schemes and squadron emblems players pick under the ship select. Lock a livery to a forum role (Pilots, Wing Leaders, Command), or give it automatically to everyone in that role. Emblems go on the hull spot you mark in the Markers editor, or on top of the hull if there's none. Ships that keep their own look (…Baked materials) only take the emblem.

Mission editor

Build new missions on a map: where pilots start, relay towers to defend, turrets, enemy waves and a convoy route, then set objectives, a time limit and bonus goals. Missions you switch on appear in the lobby. Set one to "event only" to run it as a limited mission from the Events page.

Environments

Change the worlds without code. Space missions can use a panorama backdrop (an equirectangular image, 2:1, such as 4096×2048). The planet can take your own terrain textures and a heightmap that reshapes the land.

Sounds & music

Upload MP3, OGG, WAV or M4A files in the Media library, then pick them here. Anything you leave on "Built-in" keeps the game's synthesized sound. Music loops; the menu track starts on the player's first click.

Scheduled events

Events switch things on and off by themselves between a start and an end time: a double-score weekend, a seasonal livery, a limited mission. The server checks every few seconds, so nothing needs to be saved at the start or end. A manual override on the Feature flags page still wins.

Game tuning

Change how the game plays and looks, then press Save. Every open game picks it up within a second: no redeploy. Ship, weapon and enemy changes count from the next shot or spawn; skies and nebulae from the next mission launch. Each save is kept in History, so you can roll back.

Feature flags

Switch missions, ships and events on or off for everyone, instantly — no redeploy. Players see changes within seconds; missions already in flight finish with the rules they started with.
