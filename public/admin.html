Ships & models

Swap a ship's 3D model for one from the media library. Players load it the next time they open the game. The inspector shows what the game needs: engine markers (engine_*), gun markers (gun_*), size and materials.

How to prepare a model in Blender
  1. Model the ship with its nose pointing along Blender's +Y axis, about 12–15 m long, origin in the middle.
  2. Add empties named engine_L, engine_R… at each exhaust (custom property plumeRadius sets the plume size) and gun_0, gun_1… at the cannon muzzles.
  3. Bake your materials into one material whose name ends in Baked to keep its look; otherwise name materials …Hull, …Accent (player colour), …Dark, …Canopy, …Glow.
  4. Export glTF Binary (.glb) with +Y up, “Custom Properties” on, and no Draco compression. Upload it to the media library.
